Pet-Atlas Application 
 
- As simple as any pet owner can use it without complications or confusion  
- IOS/Android 
- Linked and Synchronized with the website and should reflect any activity 
automatically 
- Anyone can use the application but only the subscribers have the access to 
the community 
- The community part should look like the WhatsApp – for the sake of 
customer peace and comfortability. 
- The subscription part includes ( Vet chat + Offers/Discounts )  
- The subscription should be set on Auto-renewal ; means that once the 
customer subscribe first time, the deduction of the cost amount should be 
automatically deducted from the customer bank account and the customer 
doesn’t have to renew it by him/her self manually…Exactly like ( Youtube 
premium subscription ) and once the client cancel the subscription or the 
process of the payment failed, the access of the community should be 
removed but still have access to the rest of the items on the application.  
- The application is dual language ( Arabic and English )  
- In the Pet-Atlas Community :- 
- Communication in the community will be through texting, sending voice 
messages and recording videos only ( No direct calls )  
- Announcement group for only announcements and members don’t have 
access to interact or comment. 
- Community group for all the members and vet account to interact, 
comment,..etc 
- Vet Chat Account is the consultant/Vet account where all the members can 
text, send videos and voice messages directly anytime with no 
appointments. 
- The access of Vet Chat Account can be given and opened from multiple 
different places at the same time.
